## 2025-05-15 - [Accessible Expandable Rows & Navigation Drawers]
**Learning:** For interactive elements that use generic `div` or `section` tags with `onclick` handlers, it is crucial to add `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, and `aria-expanded` to ensure they are discoverable and usable by screen reader and keyboard users. Additionally, for mobile navigation drawers that are hidden off-screen (e.g., via `transform: translateX`), the drawer itself must have `visibility: hidden` when closed and `visibility: visible` when open. This prevents keyboard users from accidentally tabbing into hidden menu links and prevents screen readers from announcing hidden content.
**Action:** Apply `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, and `aria-expanded` to all custom interactive components. Always use `visibility` or `display: none` for hidden modal/drawer overlays to ensure proper accessibility tree management.
